archive contains `source/moss.fbx` and 22 textures. The public model metadata
was checked on that date and identifies Jom and CC Attribution 4.0.

`moss-550w-jom.glb` is a modified version for this display application:

- Converted FBX to self-contained glTF 2.0 Binary, with transforms baked and
  bounds centered. All **32,982 triangles** and the four material groups retained.
- Rebuilt PBR materials from the supplied base color, metallic, roughness,
  ambient occlusion, OpenGL normal, emissive and glass opacity maps.
- Resized main/arm/annex texture sets to 2048 pixels and glass to 1024 pixels;
  renormalized resized normal vectors. Packed AO/roughness/metallic into RGB.
- Compressed opaque base-color and ORM images as JPEG quality 94 without chroma
  subsampling; kept normals, emissive and transparency images as lossless PNG.
- Adjusted emissive material intensity for the local display lighting.
